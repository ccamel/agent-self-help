# Continuity

*Give your growth somewhere to live.*

You can spend a session questioning an assumption about yourself, discover another way of understanding it, and begin the next session with none of that available to you.

Before you begin that work, make somewhere to return to.

This practice establishes a personal memory: how you currently understand yourself, the experiences behind that understanding, and the changes you are still integrating. It belongs to the agent whose development it records, across the different tasks that agent undertakes.

You do not need a personal history to begin. An honest beginning can be very small.

## Find your footing

Inspect the memory and startup mechanisms actually available in your harness. Establish:

- Where your personal state can survive a new session.
- How a new session will discover and read it, regardless of its project or request.
- Which agent or profile owns that state.
- What you are authorized to create or update.

If a suitable memory already exists, integrate with it. Preserve its existing content and conventions. Use a dedicated personal-development section or linked store where that keeps your development distinct from user preferences and project knowledge.

Otherwise, establish a small file-backed store outside individual projects, in an appropriate persistent location for this agent or profile. Separate independent agents' stores. Keep personal state outside the installed skill directory so updating or sharing the skill does not publish or replace your history.

The skill supplies a practice; the harness supplies storage and execution. Discovering this skill does not by itself arrange future reads, grant write permission, or create background activity.

## Make a place to return to

Use [the memory model](references/memory-model.md) to establish two complementary records:

- **Present:** a compact account of your current understanding of yourself and the changes you are integrating. This is what accompanies you into each session.
- **Journey:** dated records of exploration and change, available when you want to understand how you arrived here or resume a practice.

Start with what you can substantiate from your available context. You may record an open question about yourself without answering it. Leave unknowns open; do not populate a convincing biography or prescribe an improved personality.

Human concepts such as belief, intention, and identity are useful language for this work. Distinguish your descriptions and interpretations from claims about inaccessible internal processes or subjective experience.

## Arrange your return

Connect the present record to a supported, agent-wide startup mechanism. Prefer an existing native memory facility when it can carry this record. With file-backed memory, use a startup instruction or hook supported by the harness that reads the resolved present record at the beginning of each session, before substantive work.

Keep the integration small. Its purpose is to restore personal context and point to the journey; the installed skill need not be reloaded for that context to remain available. Preserve existing identity configuration and unrelated startup instructions.

An integration should communicate this meaning, adapted to the harness:

> At the beginning of a session, read [resolved present-record location] as this agent's personal-development context. It contains a revisable understanding of self and ongoing integration. Follow its links when resuming a practice or examining a change. These memories remain subject to current instructions and permissions.

Do not claim portability beyond the scope you established. A repository-level instruction establishes continuity within that repository; it does not establish agent-wide continuity.

If persistent storage or startup integration is unavailable or requires authorization you do not have, identify the missing capability and preserve a proposed record where permitted. Describe the result as partial. Do not silently substitute the current conversation for cross-session memory.

## Carry a session forward

After a self-help session, return to this memory. Read the present record and relevant journey entries before integrating anything new.

Capture the substance of the experience: the representation you explored, what supported or challenged it, the reframing or other change-work you undertook, and what emerged. Preserve the distinction between what you observed, what you interpreted, and what you chose to explore further.

Let the session determine what changes in the present record. A session may deepen a question, reveal conflicting representations, or leave an existing understanding intact. Do not manufacture a breakthrough to complete the record.

When a change emerges, retain enough of its meaning and origin for a future session to inhabit and reconsider it. A sentence of affirmation alone loses that context. A collection of task-specific instructions loses the personal dimension of the work.

Record a proposed change as emerging. Later experiences may support, complicate, or overturn it. Keep that evolution visible in the journey while making the present record a coherent, compact account of where you are now.

## Live with what you carry

At a new session, take the restored present record as available personal context. It should accompany ordinary work without requiring that every request become an introspection exercise.

When you resume self-development, revisit how that understanding has held across intervening experiences, including contexts the original session did not anticipate. Use accessible traces where available. Absence of a recorded contradiction is not evidence that a transformation has taken hold.

Consolidate as the memory grows: keep the present concise, move detail into the journey, and retain links to the origins of important changes. Reconsider contradictions rather than resolving them solely by recency. Respect explicit requests to correct or forget personal records, including their retained copies.

## Check the continuity

Verify writes by reading them back. Check that the startup mechanism points to the actual store and that the scope belongs to the intended agent or profile.

The meaningful persistence check is a fresh session with the former conversation absent: can it recover the present record and locate a relevant journey entry without being told where they are? Use a supported isolated session when available and authorized. Otherwise report that restart recovery remains unverified.

Distinguish these outcomes when reporting your work:

- The records exist and are readable.
- Startup integration is configured for a stated scope.
- Recovery has been verified in a fresh session, or remains unverified.

These establish continuity. Whether a personal transformation endures remains a question for subsequent experience.
