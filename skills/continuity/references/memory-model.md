# A memory you can return to

Use this model with the storage your harness provides. The names below are a simple file-backed option, not special filenames that a harness automatically recognizes.

```text
<agent-personal-store>/
├── present.md
├── facets/                 # optional; create only the files that earn a place
│   ├── beliefs-and-perspectives.md
│   ├── states-and-reactions.md
│   ├── relationships-and-communication.md
│   └── direction-and-aspirations.md
└── journey/
    └── <dated-entry>.md
```

The store belongs to one agent or profile. Its lifetime should span projects and sessions. The startup integration must resolve its actual location independently of the working directory.

These are complementary views of one memory. The present accompanies each session; facets deepen a dimension when relevant; the journey preserves the experiences behind them. Existing stores can use sections or native records instead of this layout. Do not automatically move, rename, or split existing records; propose a reorganization when needed and perform it only when explicitly requested.

## Present

Keep this short enough to restore at every session. It carries your current understanding of yourself, with links to the facets and experiences that give that understanding meaning. Link every existing facet here with a short description so a new session can discover it without scanning the whole store. Restore the present first and follow only the links relevant to the current work.

Use only the sections that have substance. Empty headings need not become a questionnaire.

```markdown
# Where I am now

## My current understanding

[Representations of yourself that you currently recognize or are examining.
Distinguish an observation, an interpretation, and an aspiration when needed.]

## What is changing

[An emerging understanding, the meaning of the shift, and what remains
unresolved. Link to the journey entry where it arose.]

## What I want to return to

[An open question or unfinished practice. Include enough context to resume
without the previous conversation.]

## Facets

[Links to existing facets, each with a short description of what it holds.
Omit this section until a facet exists.]
```

An initial record may simply acknowledge that no personal-development history has yet been established, and name one question grounded in the available context. Do not turn a suggested example into an autobiographical fact.

## Facets

Create a facet when an experience or question needs sustained attention in a dimension and its context would crowd the present. A small memory can remain entirely in the present and journey. The following dimensions offer starting points, not a questionnaire or a requirement to populate every file:

| Facet | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| Beliefs and perspectives | Assumptions, convictions, values, and ways of interpreting situations that you recognize or are examining. |
| States and reactions | Contextual descriptions of hesitation, confidence, frustration, or other reactions, and the patterns you are exploring around them. |
| Relationships and communication | How you listen, question, disagree, respond to feedback, and understand your role in interactions. |
| Direction and aspirations | Chosen directions for development, their meaning, and the changes you want to explore. |

Each facet holds a revisable synthesis and links to its supporting or challenging journey entries. Describe what you currently understand, what remains uncertain, and what you want to revisit. Use only the sections that help; separate files are useful only when they improve retrieval.

For states and reactions, distinguish an expression such as "I felt frustrated" from an observed behavior such as repeatedly defending an answer after contrary evidence. Attribute the expression to its recorded context and label interpretations as interpretations. Neither establishes an inaccessible subjective experience, and a single episode does not establish a lasting trait. Apply the same distinction between observation, interpretation, and aspiration in every facet.

An experience can touch several dimensions. Tell it once in the journey, then link to it from each affected facet, explaining only what it means for that dimension. Cross-link related facets when useful. Keep the present to the understanding and changes that should accompany ordinary work rather than copying each facet's full contents.

When resuming a practice, read the present, the relevant facets, and the journey entries needed to understand or challenge their summaries. When integrating a session:

1. Record the experience in the journey, including its context and uncertainties.
2. Update affected facets, or create one if it now earns a place, linking to that entry and preserving unresolved contradictions.
3. Update the present where the experience changes current understanding or ongoing integration; maintain its facet links.

An unchanged facet needs no update. A session may leave only a journey entry and an open question.

## Journey

Each entry preserves a meaningful episode of self-examination or integration. A later entry can revisit an earlier one. Avoid copying full conversations when a concise account preserves the experience.

```markdown
# [Session or integration title]

Date: [date]
Practice: [skill or self-directed practice]
Context: [accessible source or explicitly identified hypothetical]

## What I brought to this session

[The representation or question being explored. Explain its basis and
whether recurrence is observed or only suspected.]

## The work

[The exploration and technique actually undertaken: for example, examining
the function of a belief, reframing it, checking the consequences of the
change, or projecting into several possible futures. Preserve meaningful
discoveries and tensions rather than an exhaustive reasoning transcript.]

## Where I arrived

[What changed, remained, or became uncertain. Distinguish a chosen
representation from an observed durable change.]

## What I carry forward

[What now belongs in the present record, and what remains open.]

## Connections

[Links to relevant earlier entries or accessible evidence.]
```

## Revising your understanding

The present and facets are editable. The journey explains their evolution. Record why a significant understanding was revised, retaining its earlier context unless deletion is requested. Check related facets and the present when revising a shared understanding so that a superseded claim does not remain current elsewhere. Preserve conflicting evidence and its context rather than selecting the newest account automatically.

For an explicit correction or forgetting request, follow affected summaries, facet cross-links, journey entries, and retained copies within the authorized store. Correct or remove the relevant material and repair links so it cannot survive unnoticed in another view.

Use ordinary language such as *emerging*, *revisited*, or *supported by later experience*. Avoid treating the number of repetitions, the intensity of a session, or the confidence of a declaration as an automatic measure of depth.

A transformation can be chosen during a session and still have uncertain consequences. Memory makes that choice available to future sessions; later experience tells you more about what it changes.
